## Motivating Question

What problem forces this concept to exist? What goes wrong, or what can't
you answer, without it?

## Reasoning / Derivation

Walk through the chain of thought that gets you from the motivating
question to the concept — as if figuring it out yourself, not stating a
result. Show the false starts if they're instructive. If there's a formula,
derive it here rather than dropping it in fully formed.

## Formal Statement

Now state it precisely — definition and/or formula, each symbol explained.
This should feel like a summary of the reasoning above, not new information.

## Worked Example

A concrete example, with the reasoning shown at each step (not just the
arithmetic). Prefer numbers you compute alongside Claude over numbers
copied from a source.
